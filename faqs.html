Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before the first conversation.

Is The Finance Engineers an accounting firm?

No. We are a finance transformation consultancy. Our focus is on how the finance function operates and how it can be improved.

Are you an IT consultancy?

No. Technology is an important part of modern finance transformation, but we start with the finance and business requirements rather than with a particular system.

Do we need to replace our finance system?

Not necessarily. Often there is significant value available from improving processes and making better use of systems a business already owns.

Do you work with AI?

Yes, where it is useful. We look at where AI and automation could remove repetitive work. But we won't recommend AI simply because it is available.

What happens next?

Will you replace our finance team?

No. The objective is to help the finance function work better.

Do you implement finance systems?

It depends on the project. We can support the finance side of system selection and implementation, working alongside technology partners where needed.

Where do we start?

Usually with a conversation about the business, where finance is today and what is creating the biggest frustration or constraint.
