Who we work with

Growing businesses whose finance function needs to catch up with the rest of the organisation.

Typically owner-managed or PE-backed businesses that have grown quickly, added complexity, or reached a point where the current finance setup is starting to constrain the business.

Growing commercial businesses

Where finance processes, reporting and systems have not kept pace with the rest of the organisation.

Businesses preparing for systems change

Including ERP or finance system selection, where the underlying processes and requirements need to be understood first.

Businesses under new ownership or leadership

Where investors, boards or new management need a clearer view of how finance actually operates.

We are particularly useful where:

The finance team is working hard but still struggling to produce timely, useful information.
There is a sense that finance could be more efficient, but it is not clear where to start.
A new system is being considered, but the business is not yet sure what it needs.
Automation or AI is being discussed, but nobody is confident about the practical use cases.
