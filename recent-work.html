Preparing a growing commercial business for ERP

An example of the practical finance transformation experience behind The Finance Engineers.

The challenge

A UK commercial scaffolding business was relying on spreadsheet-based processes that were not giving management the information it needed. The business was also considering an ERP system and wanted to understand what the future system would need to support.

What we did

We reviewed and redesigned the underlying processes and built estimation, project management and commercial reporting models.

The outcome

The models are now live and in use. They also provide a proof of concept for the future ERP requirement, helping the business define what it needs before committing to a system.

Better processes first. A clearer ERP requirement second.
