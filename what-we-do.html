What we do

Finance function diagnostic and review

We start by understanding how finance operates today and what the business will need from it tomorrow.

Depending on the business, we can look across areas including:

Purchase to pay

How purchases are requested, approved, received, invoiced, paid and reconciled.

Order to cash

How sales become invoices, how revenue is recorded, how cash is collected and how outstanding debt is managed.

Month end and financial controls

The close process, reconciliations, controls, dependencies and opportunities to make month end faster and more reliable.

Management information and reporting

Whether the business is receiving the information it needs to understand performance, profitability, cash and future outlook.

Looking across systems, automation and the finance team

The review considers how all parts of the finance function work together, not each area in isolation.

Finance systems and technology

How existing systems are being used, where information is duplicated and how different applications interact.

Automation and AI

Where repetitive finance work could sensibly be automated and where AI could add genuine value.

Finance team structure

Whether roles, responsibilities, skills and capacity are right for the business today and for its next stage of growth.
